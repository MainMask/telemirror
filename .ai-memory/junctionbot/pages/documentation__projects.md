Projects help **bring order to your forwardings and digests** by **grouping** them by topic. This lets you manage whole content categories separately from each other.

**Why projects?**

- **Thematic grouping:** separate work forwardings from personal news or crypto signals.
- **Convenient navigation:** quickly switch between different workspaces in the menu.
- **Focus on what matters:** view lists of only those chats relevant to the current task.

### Getting started (Как начать)

Projects are in the **side menu (burger)** of the mini-app. You can switch between the "All projects" (`Все проекты`) view and each project separately.

So you can start right away, the system already has **Project #1** (`Проект #1`) created (you can rename it).

### How to put a forwarding or digest into a project (Как положить пересылку или дайджест в проект)

You can easily move your settings between projects to keep things tidy:

- Go to the **My forwardings** (`Мои пересылки`) or **My digests** (`Мои дайджесты`) list.
- Tap the **three dots (⋮)** next to the item.
- Choose **Move to project** (`Переместить в проект`).
- In the list that opens, choose the target and tap **Select** (`Выбрать`).

If you have not yet created the needed project, you can do it right from the move dialog by tapping "**+ Create new project**" (`+ Создать новый проект`).

The new project appears in your list immediately, and you can switch to it via the burger menu.

### Viewing the list, editing and deleting (Просмотр списка, редактирование и удаление)

To see the whole structure of your automation and make changes, use the management screen:

- **Project list:** open the burger menu and choose **Manage projects** (`Управление проектами`). Here you see all your projects with counters of forwardings and digests.
- **Create a new project:** tap **Create new project**, enter a name and save.
- **Edit:** tap the three dots (⋮) on a project and choose **Edit** (`Редактировать`). You can change the name.
- **Delete:** three dots (⋮) on a project → **Edit** → **Delete project** (`Удалить проект`). Only an empty project can be deleted: if forwardings or digests remain in it, first move them to another project.
