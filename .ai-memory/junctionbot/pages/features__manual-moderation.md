# Manual Moderation

## Use Junction Bot's pre-moderation mode to improve the quality of your Telegram channel's content

**Automatic forwarding of content between Telegram channels is convenient, but often leads to lower quality of publications.** Irrelevant posts, typos and unsuitable formatting can repel subscribers and blur the channel's concept.

**Junction Bot offers an effective solution to this problem — the pre-moderation (премодерация) feature.** It lets you carefully control every post before publication while keeping the convenience of automation.

#### You
- **review** the posts proposed for publication
- **make the necessary edits** to the text and media
- **schedule the release time** of materials
- **reject** unsuitable content

#### The bot
- **applies the changes made**
- **publishes** approved content at the specified time

Pre-moderation is a mode of manual control and editing of messages before their automatic publication.

### DEFERRED PUBLICATION (ОТЛОЖЕННАЯ ПУБЛИКАЦИЯ)
