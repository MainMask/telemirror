1. **Choose chats** — open @junction_bot in Telegram, tap "Create digest" (Создать дайджест) and add channels and chats to monitor (public or private).
2. **AI reads everything (automatically)** — Junction Bot monitors your channels 24/7. AI reads hundreds of messages, extracts only what matters and organizes it by topics.
3. **Get the digest** — delivered to any Telegram chat. Only the most important. Read in 5 minutes instead of 3 hours.

### Only JUNCTION BOT: tell the AI what exactly to extract
Junction Bot is the only solution that lets you decide what exactly to extract from chats and channels. Use cases:
- 💼 **Looking for leads in niche communities** — Task: find people with problems your product solves, or unhappy with competitors. Example prompt: "Покажи жалобы и проблемы с [тема вашего продукта]. Выдели фразы 'не работает', 'сложно', 'дорого', 'ищу альтернативу'. Обсуждения конкурентов с негативом показывай отдельно." (EN: "Show complaints and problems with [your product topic]. Highlight phrases 'doesn't work', 'difficult', 'expensive', 'looking for an alternative'. Show negative discussions of competitors separately.")
- 💬 **Drowning in work chats** — Task: a general summary or a topic-specific selection from dozens of discussions. Example prompt: "Суммируй главные решения и задачи с упоминанием меня. Покажи дедлайны и вопросы, требующие ответа. Мемы и нерелевантные темы убери." (EN: "Summarize the main decisions and tasks mentioning me. Show deadlines and questions requiring an answer. Remove memes and irrelevant topics.")

All other digest tools give generic summaries from public sources. Only Junction Bot works with private sources and lets you control what the AI extracts and how it formats results.

### How to create a digest in 4 steps
A digest is a short AI-composed summary of news from the Telegram chats you need.
1. **Choose sources** — specify public or private chats to get the digest from.
2. **Configure the format** — pick a ready digest template or write your own prompt for the ideal structure.
3. **Set the schedule** — mark the days and times when you want to receive the latest summary.
4. **Specify the target** — choose one or more chats where the finished digest will be sent.

All set! The digest is configured and will be sent exactly on schedule. Ideal for work and study processes, information and news feeds, and client communication. Example digests: @markettwits_digest (https://t.me/markettwits_digest).
