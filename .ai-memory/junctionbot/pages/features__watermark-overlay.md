# Watermark Overlay

## Use Junction Bot to automatically overlay watermarks on images and videos to protect content from misuse

**In today's digital space, content is easily copied and distributed without permission.** This is especially relevant in Telegram, where any media files are easily copied and forwarded. If you create unique images or videos, you need to protect your intellectual property.

**Junction Bot offers a feature for automatically overlaying watermarks on all images and videos forwarded through it. This protects your content and helps avoid unauthorized use.**

#### YOU
- **Upload** one or several images for the watermark
- **Define** the transparency and configure overlay parameters
- Choose in which forwardings to use watermarks

#### BOT
- **Processes** all images and videos passing through it
- **Automatically applies the watermark** according to the specified parameters
- **Forwards** the protected media content to the target chat or channel

[Image: "Watermark overlay"]

The watermark overlay feature lets you automatically add your logo, name or link to media files forwarded through the bot.
